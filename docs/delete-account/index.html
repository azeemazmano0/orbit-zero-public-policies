Delete your Orbit Zero account

Last updated: 11 November 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your Orbit Zero account and associated data whether or not the app is installed on your device.

Request account deletion by email

How to request deletion

  1. Email orbitzero.askme@gmail.com, preferably from the email registered to your Orbit Zero account.
  2. Use the subject Orbit Zero Account Deletion Request. Clearly state that you want the account permanently deleted.
  3. Our support operator will verify account ownership and may request a confirmation reply or a separate verification step. If you have lost access to the registered email, explain this so an alternative verification method can be assessed.
  4. Once verified, we will process the request and notify you when deletion is complete.

Never send your password, access tokens, recovery codes or identity-document scans. An unverified request will not result in account deletion.

Data covered by deletion

Deletion is intended to remove your active account, profile, Passport stamps, achievement eligibility, associated adventures, visits and other personal records linked to that account. If user-owned uploads exist, those need to be removed from active storage as part of fulfillment. Public, shared destination catalogue information that is not your personal data is not deleted.

Information that may remain temporarily

Some information may remain for legitimate security, legal and operational reasons: a minimal record that the deletion request was fulfilled, support correspondence, security logs, limited recovery exports and provider-managed backups. Support correspondence and temporary recovery exports are reviewed for disposal when no longer needed for the stated purpose, subject to applicable legal or security requirements. Specific operational retention periods are being finalized. Provider-hosted logs and backups follow the applicable retention controls; older copies may not be removed individually on demand.

What happens to access

Deleting your account blocks new sign-ins and token refresh after processing. An already-issued access token may remain technically valid until it expires, depending on the protected service and session checks. We do not promise immediate expiration of every previously issued token.

Reset All Achievements is different

Reset All Achievements clears your exploration progress without removing your Orbit Zero login. To delete the account, use the request method on this page.
