Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Orbit Zero account even if you no longer have the app.

Email a deletion request

How to request

  1. Email orbitzero.askme@gmail.com with the subject Orbit Zero account deletion request.
  2. Use your registered email if accessible. If not, explain that you need an alternative verification method.
  3. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or sensitive identity documents.

Ownership verification

To protect accounts, support must verify control of the account before deleting it. An email or display name alone does not prove ownership.

What will be removed

After verification and authorized processing, the intended scope includes the authentication account, profile, Passport stamps, visit and adventure history and related active-service data. Verification pending: actual fulfillment test, photo cleanup and provider retention details.

What may remain temporarily

Justified security records, backups, recovery exports or support correspondence may remain temporarily under documented controls and legal requirements. Review pending: actual retention schedules and disposal procedures.

Reset is different

Reset All Achievements removes journey progress but does not delete the account.

Confirmation

Support will confirm after a verified deletion has been completed. The request mailbox and full fulfillment process still require end-to-end acceptance before this page is published.
