Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Orbit Zero is an exploration and digital Passport app. This policy explains the information we handle, why it is needed and how you can request its deletion. This policy covers the app and its supporting services.

Information we process

  • Account and profile: your registered email, account identifier, optional profile details and authentication-related records.
  • Exploration and Passport: destinations, check-in eligibility, adventures, verified visits, earned stamps, achievement state and related timestamps.
  • Location: location information needed to evaluate a GPS-based check-in when you choose to use that feature. Location-related verification events and metadata may be recorded. Access to location is controlled by app and device permissions.
  • Photos and other contributions: images and related information when a photo-upload feature is offered and you choose to submit content.
  • Support and technical information: information included in your messages and limited security or diagnostic events needed to investigate problems.

Why we use this information

We use account information to authenticate you and maintain your profile; exploration data to verify visits and display Passport achievements; optional content to support destination features; and support/diagnostic data to troubleshoot, protect accounts and operate the service.

Services and sharing

Orbit Zero uses Supabase for authentication and backend services and may be distributed through Google Play. Support email services process messages you send. These providers may process information outside your country. We do not publish your personal account information as an open dataset or sell account data to advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Active account records and associated Passport or adventure data are generally kept while your account is in use. You can request permanent deletion at any time using our Delete Account page, including after uninstalling the app. We verify ownership before deleting the account and associated active-service data.

Limited exceptions: support correspondence, operational/security logs, recovery exports and provider-managed backups may be retained temporarily where necessary for security, legal requirements, troubleshooting or documented recovery. Our standard internal disposal targets are 90 days after a support case closes for ordinary support messages, and 30 days after creation for temporary recovery exports, subject to a justified exception. Provider-hosted backups and logs follow their verified retention and rotation controls; they do not disappear immediately when an account is deleted. If restored data contains a previously deleted account, we must reapply its deletion before allowing that data back into active service.

Resetting progress is not deleting your account

Reset All Achievements can clear Passport progress while keeping your login. To remove your account and associated data, use the account-deletion request.

Your choices

You can control optional permissions in your device settings. For access, correction or deletion questions, contact us at orbitzero.askme@gmail.com. Do not send your password or access tokens.

Updates

We may update this policy if features or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
