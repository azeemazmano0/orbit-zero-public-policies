Privacy Policy

Drafted 10 November 2026. Effective date not yet approved.

Orbit Zero helps explorers discover places, record verified visits, and collect Passport achievements. This page is a working description of data practices, not a final disclosure.

Information associated with accounts

  • Account identifiers, registration email and authentication details.
  • Profile information supplied by the explorer.
  • Destination selections, adventures, verified visits, Passport stamps and related dates.
  • Device location used to assess a GPS check-in where enabled. Verification pending: whether raw GPS samples or location history are retained.
  • Photos or other content where upload features are enabled. Verification pending: visibility, storage and disposal.
  • Support and security/diagnostic records. Verification pending: exact categories and collection settings.

Purposes

These records support account access, destinations, check-in eligibility, Passport synchronization, support requests and security.

Providers

Orbit Zero uses Supabase for authentication and backend data. Google Play may distribute the Android app. Verification pending: complete service-provider list, optional analytics/crash tools, and applicable international processing.

Retention and deletion

Account records, Passport stamps, adventures and visits are retained for account operation and are intended to be removed from the active service following a verified deletion request, except where justified by a specific lawful requirement. Reset All Achievements is separate.

Proposed operator policy, not yet implemented or approved: close and dispose of ordinary support correspondence 90 days after the support case closes, and delete temporary recovery exports within 30 days of creation or earlier when no longer necessary. Exception handling must be narrowly documented, access-restricted and reviewed.

Supabase service logs and backup snapshots are subject to provider-controlled plan settings, not a fixed Orbit Zero deletion deadline. For example, the Supabase Free plan ordinarily exposes one day of service logs and does not include Pro daily-backup availability; plans and settings may change. Verification pending: current billed plan, optional database-stored Auth audit logs, whether independent exports/backups exist, and any restoration safeguard for previously deleted users.

A verified account deletion request must be fulfilled and checked before a completion confirmation is sent. No immediate revocation of already-issued access tokens is promised without separate testing.

Reset versus deletion

Account deletion is separate from Reset All Achievements, which retains the login.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests: orbitzero.askme@gmail.com. Never send passwords or access tokens. Verification pending: legal operator identification and disclosures.
